Problem Statement:

Modern internet culture moves at lightning speed, yet users struggle with fragmented social media feeds where viral memes, breaking cultural moments, and niche humour get buried under generic algorithms, ads and irrelevant personal updates. Meanwhile, meme creators lack a dedicated, community-driven space with intuitive categorisation, real-time engagement mechanics, and transparent trending leaderboards to showcase their work and build a recognised identity.

Buzzboard addresses this by providing a unified, fast-paced hub tailored for discovering, reacting to, and curating viral content, free from the noise of traditional social networks.
